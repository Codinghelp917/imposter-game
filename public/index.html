How to play
  1. Everyone sees the same secret word. The imposter only sees the category — they're faking it.
  2. Take turns in the chat dropping one clue about the word: subtle enough to hide it, clear enough to prove you're not the imposter.
  3. After each round you can vote someone out — or skip to keep discussing. You get 3 rounds per imposter.
  4. Eject a crewmate → they're out, the game rolls on. Eject the imposter → crew win!
