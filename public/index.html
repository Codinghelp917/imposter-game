Party word game · 3+ players

One of you is the imposter.

Everyone gets the same secret word — except the imposter, who's flying on a vague hint. Drop your clues, then vote out the faker. Catch them and they still get one last shot at naming the word.

How to play
  1. Everyone sees the same secret word. The imposter only gets a vague hint — they're faking it.
  2. Take turns dropping one clue each: subtle enough to hide the word, clear enough to prove you know it.
  3. After each round you vote someone out — or skip. You get 3 rounds per imposter.
  4. Eject a crewmate → they're out, the game rolls on.
  5. Eject the imposter → they get one last guess at the word. Name it and the imposters steal the win.
  6. A living imposter can also gamble a guess at any time. Right = instant win. Wrong = they're out.
